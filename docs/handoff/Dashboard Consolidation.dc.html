Amendment plan · Register → Dashboard
One landing page that answers Monday's question — what needs to happen this week, and where's the risk.
1 · Routing. New #/dashboard view becomes the default landing for everyone (replaces Projects as home). The register view key and #/register route stay alive as the drill-down "Open register" page, so nothing deep-linked breaks.
2 · Ribbon. REGISTER tab is retired; DASHBOARD takes its slot, first in the nav. The dashboard summarises; every card links into the full register, filtered.
3 · Role gating. Same page, gated sections — reuses isSenior. Contributors: their week, their projects, activity. Execs add project health, accountability, pipeline, programme strip, unscoped audit log. This IS the exec dashboard from Phase 7 — no second page later.
4 · Activity & audit. Activity rides along as a condensed rail/tab (bell dropdown unchanged); "View full audit log" is the auditable record. Reconcile the two audit sources (audit_log vs item_events) as part of this step — the per-row history expander below reads item_events.
5 · Sequencing. Lands as dev-plan Step 4 (accountability, registers, audit — buildable today, interim health = overdue + ageing flags + completeness). Step 7 later swaps in composite health and value-weighted pipeline into the same slots — greyed terms are shown where they'll appear.
Three concepts, right — each has a Contributor / Exec toggle, working tabs, and click-to-expand audit history on register rows. 1a briefing-led (recommended) · 1b boards-led · 1c minimal change.
